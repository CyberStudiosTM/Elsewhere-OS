## ୨୧ welcome to elsewhere ☁️

remember when computers felt a little more magical?

when desktops had personality, windows were glossy, everything was blue and shiny, and opening a messaging app felt like stepping into a whole other world?

**Elsewhere OS** is a nostalgic, early-2000s-inspired desktop experience built around that feeling.

inspired by the charm of classic Microsoft interfaces, Frutiger Aero, and the wonderfully personal internet of the 2000s, Elsewhere brings those familiar feelings into a modern environment.

it's not about replacing your operating system. it's about creating a little world inside it.

> ♡ the internet used to feel like a place. let's make it feel that way again.

---

## 🫧 what is elsewhere os?

Elsewhere OS is a standalone, Windows-inspired desktop environment designed to feel like a computer from another timeline.

think fluffy clouds, translucent windows, nostalgic system sounds, little desktop applications, and the kind of interface you would've spent hours customizing in 2005.

a place to explore, personalize, create, and just exist for a while.

### ✧ the vision

- **a nostalgic desktop** with movable windows, classic menus, a taskbar, and familiar interactions.
- **frutiger aero aesthetics** featuring sky blues, pearly whites, glossy surfaces, and dreamy desktop backgrounds.
- **personalization** through wallpapers, colors, sounds, and display settings.
- **little desktop applications** that feel like they belong inside their own miniature computer universe.
- **elsewhere messenger**, a companion messaging experience inspired by MSN and Yahoo! Messenger.
- **a cozy digital atmosphere** where even the smallest interactions have personality.

---

## 💿 elsewhere messenger

**your little corner of the internet. ♡**

Elsewhere Messenger is a related project inspired by the social side of early-2000s computing.

classic buddy lists, customizable chat windows, status messages, nudges, and a whole lot of nostalgic personality.

the broader design also includes **Elsewhere Photo Studio**, a purikura-inspired decorating workspace for photos, stickers, frames, digital stationery, and other little internet treasures.

Messenger and Photo Studio are separate development efforts, designed with future Elsewhere OS integration in mind.
