## ☁️ ୨୧ welcome to elsewhere

remember when computers felt magical?

when everything was glossy, windows were translucent, wallpapers were full of blue skies, and opening your favorite messaging app felt like stepping into an entirely different world?

**Elsewhere OS** is a love letter to that era.

inspired by the visual language of early Microsoft desktops, Frutiger Aero, and the wonderfully personal internet of the 2000s, Elsewhere reimagines the desktop as a cozy little digital universe.

it's familiar, but not quite the same.

a computer from another timeline. a place that feels like somewhere you've been before, even if you've never actually visited.

> ♡ the internet used to feel like a place. let's bring that feeling back.

---

## 🫧 what is elsewhere os?

Elsewhere OS is a Windows-inspired desktop environment designed to bring nostalgia, personality, and creativity into the modern computing experience.

**it's not intended to replace Windows.**

instead, Elsewhere is envisioned as a standalone desktop experience that exists within your existing operating system.

think nostalgic system sounds, draggable windows, little desktop programs, customizable wallpapers, and an interface that feels like it belongs inside a beautiful alternate universe.

### ✧ the vision

| Feature | Description |
|:---|:---|
| 🪟 **Desktop environment** | A familiar desktop with interactive windows, menus, and a taskbar. |
| ☁️ **Elsewhaero aesthetic** | Dreamy skies, glossy surfaces, translucent panels, and soft blue-white palettes. |
| 🎀 **Personalization** | Wallpapers, colors, system sounds, and personal preferences. |
| 💿 **Desktop applications** | Little programs and utilities that make the environment feel lived in. |
| 💌 **Elsewhere Messenger** | A nostalgic messaging companion inspired by early-2000s internet culture. |
| ✧ **A connected universe** | A growing collection of experiences designed to feel like pieces of the same world. |

*These describe the project's vision and development scope. Individual features may not yet be available in a public build.*

## 💌 elsewhere messenger

### ୨୧ your little corner of the internet.

remember buddy lists, away messages, nudges, and the excitement of seeing somebody come online?

**Elsewhere Messenger** is a nostalgic desktop messaging project inspired by MSN Messenger, Yahoo! Messenger, and the social side of the early internet.

its goal is to bring the charm of classic instant messaging into a modern application without losing the personality that made those programs special.

### ✧ the messenger experience

- nostalgic chat windows and classic messaging aesthetics
- a personalized buddy-list experience
- customizable appearances and status messages
- old-school interactions and playful details
- a growing set of creative tools and companion features

### 🎀 creative studio

**Creative Studio** (once the Photo Studio design) is now in the Messenger beta: a purikura-inspired workspace for making anything, from decorated photos to collages and stationery.

think stickers, scrapbook frames, cute stationery, photo strips, personal collections of old-web treasures, and 23 themed workspace looks. **SkinMapper** (design your own Messenger skins) and **Pixel Doll Studio** (layered pixel dress-up) live alongside it, both in beta.

all three are beta features and still growing.

### ☁️ get elsewhere messenger

Elsewhere Messenger has its own beta release available for Windows and macOS.

## 🎨 the elsewhere aesthetic

the visual direction is called **Elsewhaero**, our interpretation of the dreamy, optimistic design language associated with mid-2000s computing.

### ♡ a few ingredients

**☁️ Frutiger Aero**
  
clear skies, glossy glass, soft gradients, pearly whites, and bright blues.

**💿 Y2K computing**

classic desktop interactions, nostalgic system windows, and software that feels full of personality.

**🫧 Liminal nostalgia**

the feeling of stumbling across an old computer in a place you almost remember.

**🎀 Creative freedom**

personalization, playful details, and little opportunities to make the environment feel like your own.

the goal isn't to recreate Windows pixel for pixel.

it's to capture a feeling and build something new around it.
